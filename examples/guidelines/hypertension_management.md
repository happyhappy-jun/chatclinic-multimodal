# Hypertension in Adults

Blood pressure is categorized using the average of two or more properly measured readings on two or
more occasions. Normal is below 120/80 mmHg. Elevated blood pressure is a systolic of 120 to 129 and
a diastolic below 80. Stage 1 hypertension is 130 to 139 systolic or 80 to 89 diastolic. Stage 2
hypertension is at or above 140 systolic or 90 diastolic.

## Lifestyle modification

All patients with elevated blood pressure or hypertension should receive lifestyle counseling: weight
loss if overweight, a heart-healthy dietary pattern such as the DASH diet, sodium reduction, increased
dietary potassium, regular physical activity, and moderation of alcohol intake.

## When to start medication

Pharmacologic therapy is recommended for stage 1 hypertension when the estimated 10-year
atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease risk is 10 percent or higher, or in patients with established
cardiovascular disease, diabetes, or chronic kidney disease. For stage 2 hypertension, start two
first-line agents from different classes in most patients.

## First-line drug classes

First-line agents include thiazide or thiazide-like diuretics, ACE inhibitors, angiotensin receptor
blockers, and calcium channel blockers. ACE inhibitors and angiotensin receptor blockers should not be
combined. In Black adults without heart failure or chronic kidney disease, initial therapy more often
includes a calcium channel blocker or a thiazide-type diuretic.

## Target

A common treatment target is a blood pressure below 130/80 mmHg for most adults, individualized to
comorbidity and tolerability.
